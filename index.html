About SQC

Super Quant Computing (SQC) is a student-driven technical and quality-focused initiative at NIT Jalandhar. It promotes excellence in advanced computing, innovation, and quality management.

The club provides a collaborative platform for students to work on real-world problems, research ideas, and emerging technologies.

Founder

Super Quant Computing was founded under the guidance of dedicated faculty members of NIT Jalandhar with the vision of developing a strong culture of quality and advanced computing among students.

The founders aimed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical industry applications.

Aim of SQC

  • To enhance technical and analytical skills of students
  • To promote quality tools and continuous improvement
  • To encourage innovation and research mindset
  • To prepare students for industry and academic challenges
